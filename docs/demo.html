VALIDATEDWhat must a party show for evidence to qualify as attorney work product?14s
The two primary elements required for a document to qualify as attorney work product are that it must be a product of the attorney's mental processes and made in anticipation of litigation. [99cc6f5a p.14] When seeking discovery of tangible work product materials, the requesting party must demonstrate good cause by showing the material is directly at issue, the need is compelling, and the evidence is unavailable elsewhere. [8f0f1182 p.15]
